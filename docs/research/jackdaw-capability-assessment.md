# Jackdaw capability assessment

**Assessed revision:** [`8712c1e1cf3fe3e78703b82094e5321c84c21246`](https://github.com/TylerFlar/jackdaw-balatro/tree/8712c1e1cf3fe3e78703b82094e5321c84c21246), upstream `HEAD` observed 2026-09-10.

## Decision-relevant finding

Jackdaw is a plausible simulator-training backend and supplies a bridge-shaped route to live BalatroBot validation. It does **not** presently establish that a policy trained in its simulator behaves equivalently in this repository's live `RoundTacticsEnvironment`. Adoption must therefore keep BalatroBot as the conformance authority and add a deliberately scoped simulator-to-live fidelity gate before training results are treated as live-game evidence.

## Incompatibilities with current Balatron contracts

Our `RoundTacticsEnvironment` starts only Red/White, automatically selects the first Small Blind, accepts only canonical `PLAY` and `DISCARD` card-index subsets, and terminates at round evaluation or game over. Its observation is a shop-free live projection and its reward favors clearing the blind with hands remaining. [Current contract](../../balatro-gym/balatro_gym/environments/live/round_tactics.py).

Jackdaw instead begins at blind selection, represents a full-run action space, exposes entity-array observations, and uses a 21-type factored action contract. Its Gymnasium projection dynamically enumerates to a capped flat action table. Consequently:

- Existing BalatroBot API/CLI and `RoundTacticsEnvironment` remain the narrow live-validation and watchability path; they are not invalidated.
- The deterministic legal heuristic remains a valuable baseline inside its own `RoundTactics` contract, but cannot be passed directly to Jackdaw without an intentional policy/action adapter or reimplementation.
- No existing checkpoint, policy feature layout, reward comparison, or legality assertion should be interpreted across both environments without a new adapter contract and conformance tests.

## Explicit unknowns and required adoption evidence

1. **Selected integration revision:** this assessment freezes the observed upstream `HEAD`; the project must choose and lock a commit in `uv.lock` before code relies on it.
2. **Actual current compatibility with our BalatroBot installation:** upstream source references multiple BalatroBot repository locations and its live tests were not run against this checkout's installed game/mod. We need an integration probe against our process version.
3. **Route-level fidelity:** upstream fixtures and scenarios do not demonstrate every legal action across our future training route. Establish paired seeded trajectories, comparing state projections, legal actions, terminal outcomes, and action translation at each settled decision point.
4. **Policy portability:** the assessed Jackdaw source does not currently support its own full observation/mask generation from the reduced `LiveBackend` projection. It is unknown whether a selected PPO encoding can be consumed by our live adapter without loss; assume a separate policy-serving/translation boundary is necessary unless a complete live-state translation layer is built and verified.
5. **Action completeness under the gym cap:** combinations can be randomly subsampled and the full action table is capped at 500. Determine whether valid high-branching decisions relevant to the route may be omitted and how that impacts evaluation fairness.
6. **Reward and metric alignment:** choose the training reward separately from the existing benchmark's terminal reward, then define how a trained agent is evaluated fairly against the deterministic baseline and eventual human reference.
7. **Operational evidence:** no local install, test run, benchmark, or live validation was performed for this research ticket; upstream claims are source-inspected only.

## Recommendation for the next decision

Adopt the **direct-simulator for training / existing live BalatroBot path for conformance and demonstration** direction conditionally. The next ticket should decide the responsibility boundary and a small initial fidelity gate: Red Deck, White Stake, a fixed seed partition, first Small Blind first, and only actions both systems can express. Expand into shops, packs, tags, and broader antes only after the gate passes; these are where the documented oracle limitations and action-contract mismatch become material.
